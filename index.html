A letter from my heart

To My Favourite Girl

My love,

I don't think you quite understand what you've done to my heart. Somewhere between our conversations, your silly little antics and the countless moments we've shared, you've become my favourite part of being alive.

And honestly? I wouldn't have it any other way.

❦

If You Could See What I See

I'd show you yourself through my eyes. Maybe then you'd understand why I find you so beautiful, even when you're sleepy, grumpy, messy or being the biggest nuisance imaginable.

I'd show you how your smile stays in my head long after our conversations end. How your voice can make a terrible day feel softer. How even the smallest things about you have somehow become things I adore.

God, I hope you never doubt how lovely you are, sweetheart.

❦

In Every Little Tomorrow

I don't just want the grand adventures and beautiful photographs. I want the ordinary Tuesdays, the random calls, the sleepy goodnights, the ridiculous arguments over absolutely nothing and the laughter that follows.

I want to know about your day, your dreams, your worries and every little thing you think nobody would care to hear. I want to celebrate your victories, hold your hand through the difficult bits and remind you that you never have to face everything alone.

I don't need us to be perfect. I just want us to keep choosing each other, understanding each other and growing together.

❦

A Little Promise

I promise to keep finding new reasons to fall in love with you. To never stop being curious about you. To listen when you need to be heard, to laugh with you whenever life gives us a reason, and to love you on the days when loving yourself feels a little harder.

And if the universe somehow gave me a thousand different lifetimes, I think I'd still spend every one hoping to stumble across you.

Because somewhere along the way, you became my favourite hello, my hardest goodbye and the person I wish I could steal just five more minutes with.

All my love, all my kisses,
and all the little pieces of my heart.

Yours, endlessly. ♡
